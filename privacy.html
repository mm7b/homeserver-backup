Privacy policy

homeserver-backup · last updated 23 October 2026

homeserver-backup is a personal script run by one person on their own home server. It has no users other than its author, no accounts, no sign-up, and no server of its own.

What data it handles

The script reads configuration files from the home server it runs on, packs them into a single archive, encrypts that archive, and uploads it to a folder in the author's own Google Drive. The archives are encrypted before they leave the machine, so their contents are not readable in Google Drive without the author's passphrase.

Google account access

The Google OAuth client is used only to write these archive files into, and read them back from, the author's own Google Drive, through rclone. It is never used to access anyone else's account. No Google user data is collected, stored elsewhere, transferred to third parties, sold, or used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or training models.

Other people's data

The script collects nothing from visitors of this page: no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no logs. These pages are static files served by GitHub Pages, whose own privacy statement covers whatever GitHub records when you request them.

Retention

Encrypted archives are kept in the author's Google Drive on a rolling schedule — daily copies for two weeks, weekly for ten weeks, monthly for about a year — and are deleted automatically once they fall outside that window. They can be deleted at any time by the author, who is the only person with access to them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at github.com/mm7b/homeserver-backup.
